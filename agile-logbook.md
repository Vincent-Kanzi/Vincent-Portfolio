### Extract — Sprint [N]
**Daily stand-up (Day 3):** Blocker — [dependency] pending, escalated to the

**Retrospective:** *Start* — refine backlog mid-sprint. *Stop* — updating the

## Reflection
My logbook is the artefact that most clearly shows my growth. If I compare my
early coursework entries — short, factual, "no blockers" repeated every day —
with my later internship ones, I can see I started recording *why* things
happened and what I would change. My retrospective notes trace a real arc from
blaming circumstances ("the server was down") to fixing systems (adding a
readiness checklist, adjusting our capacity).

Keeping the log also showed me a pattern I would otherwise have missed: my
blockers clustered around external dependencies, which changed how I sequenced
work in the final sprint.
