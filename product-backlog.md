## Reflection
In coursework I treated the backlog as a static to-do list. In the internship
the refinement sessions showed me it is a **living, ranked conversation** —
items get split, re-estimated and re-ordered as understanding grows. Using MoSCoW
forced me to justify priority with value rather than enthusiasm: an item I
championed like the dark mode I kept pushing for in QuizApp kept sinking,
because it served no real need in this release.

I also felt the cost of a badly refined backlog. In one sprint we pulled an item
we had never discussed and lost half the sprint to clarification. Since then I
insist on a "ready" check — clear story, acceptance criteria, an estimate —
before anything enters a sprint.
